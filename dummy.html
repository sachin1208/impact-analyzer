ℹ️ What is Impact Analysis?

Impact analysis helps you understand how a code change might affect other modules in your system. It:

  • Identifies all modules that depend on or are depended by the changed module
  • Calculates risk scores based on module criticality and relationships
  • Detects cascade effects (chain reactions) through dependencies
  • Recommends testing strategies and effort estimates
  • Generates test plans for affected modules
